# Family Stability & Relationship Operating System

## Purpose

Project Salus treats family stability, marriage health, communication, emotional intelligence, and asset protection as mission-critical domains tied to legacy, wealth preservation, health, and long-term readiness.

Relationship health is not a soft optional category. It is a core asset-protection and human-capability mission.

---

## Strategic Principle

A family is an operating system.

If the system lacks maintenance, communication, repair protocols, financial transparency, and legal clarity, it degrades over time.

Project Salus exists to detect drift early, support better judgment, and preserve family cohesion.
